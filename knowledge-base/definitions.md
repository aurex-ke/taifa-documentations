# Definitions and conventions

Record source-specific definitions alongside general working definitions.
Where a report defines a term differently, preserve the report's wording and
note the source and page; do not silently harmonize definitions.

## Working definitions

| Term | Working definition | Source / status |
|---|---|---|
| Energy Management System (EMS) | Software, with supporting hardware and data integration, that monitors, forecasts, optimizes, and/or controls energy generation, storage, and consumption. | Research brief; working scope definition, not an EPRA finding. |
| DATA | A statement directly reported in a source. | Research brief. |
| CALCULATION | A result derived from cited source values; include the formula and working. | Research brief. |
| INTERPRETATION | An analytical judgment based on evidence; explain rationale and uncertainty. | Research brief. |
| External, unverified | Information outside the two core reports that has not been independently verified for this study. | Research brief. |

## Units and conventions

Record unit conversions, abbreviations, and any conventions that affect
comparisons here. Keep original source units in evidence records and show
converted units and conversion factors explicitly.

- **Energy:** GWh and MWh as in the reports. AR Fig 2.1, 2.2 and 2.9 axes say "MWh" but the values are GWh. AR Figs 2.10 and 2.16 are genuinely MWh. BR Fig 2.7 is MWh.
- **Capacity:** MW.
- **Tariffs:** KSh/kWh. Demand charges are shown as "Ksh." with no unit stated (AR p.26 Table 2.4; BR p.20 Table 2.3).
- **Primary energy:** ktoe (AR Ch7). Any conversion to or from MWh is external, unverified.
- **Percentages vs basis points:** AR uses both. Convert explicitly.
- **Period labels:** FY-H1 = Jul–Dec 2025 (BR); FY-H2 = Jan–Jun 2026 = AR minus BR (CALCULATION). BR "H1/H2" figure labels are calendar halves (see definitions).
- **Working convention (this study):** capacity factor = GWh / (MW × 8.76); load factor = GWh / (peak MW × 8.76). Both are CALCULATIONS, not report values.
- **Evidence ID scheme:** D-nnn = DATA, C-nnn = CALCULATION, I-nnn = INTERPRETATION.
