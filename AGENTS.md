# Working instructions

- Use `research/research_brief.md` as the scope and required output contract.
- Follow `research/methodology.md`; cite report evidence precisely and
  distinguish DATA, CALCULATION, and INTERPRETATION.
- Maintain a neutral diagnosis. Do not let project, business, or open-source
  preferences determine whether a need exists.
- Do not invent figures, report metadata, or citations. Mark uncertain source
  details for verification and label outside information `external, unverified`.
- Keep original files in `data/raw/` unchanged. Put extracted material in
  `data/processed/` and preserve traceability to the source report and locator.
- Maintain the knowledge-base registers as the analysis progresses. Keep draft
  work separate from the final output.
- Do not state conclusions or solution recommendations as established facts
  before completing the required evidence review and hypothesis tests.
